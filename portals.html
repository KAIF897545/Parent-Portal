About this portal

The Official Student & Coach Portal

Maldives Chess Club — sixty-four squares, one thousand islands.

Conceived, engineered, and driven by Kaif Mausoom and Mohamed Farish.

Welcome to the digital nervous system of Maldivian chess. Built to bridge ocean gaps, connect schools across the atolls, and turn raw potential into calculated mastery.

🚀 The vision: beyond the board

From a single borrowed board to a nationwide movement, Maldives Chess Club has always believed that chess is more than a game — it is a training ground for life.

This portal is our next evolution. It is designed to dissolve the distance between students, schools, and coaches. Whether a young mind is making their very first pawn move in Malé or calculating deep endgame tactics across the atolls, this platform ensures that growth is tracked, brilliance is recognized, and mentorship is never more than a click away.
