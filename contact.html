Ask about the platform

Frequently asked

Who can sign in right now?

Students, coaches and parents whose school or programme is already set up. Use the sign-in details you were given.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It works in any browser on a phone, tablet or computer. If you'd like it on your home screen like an app, open the site on your phone and choose “Add to Home Screen”. On iPhone, it's in the Share menu. On Android, it's in the browser menu.

Is there a cost?

Our aim is to make chess accessible to every student, so our pricing is kept deliberately low. For full details, please contact us.

Do I have to use your syllabus?

No. Schools, programmes and independent coaches can all use their own. Send it to us and we'll set it up on the platform for you.

What happens after I email you?

We'll get back to you, talk through what you need, and set everything up. You'll get sign-in details for your coaches and students.
