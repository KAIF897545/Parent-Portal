Bring the portal to your students

We'll set up a portal for your school so every coach and student has their own sign-in, with progress and feedback tracked per student.

  1. 1 Email us your school's name and the age groups you teach.
  2. 2 We set up your school's portal and coach accounts.
  3. 3 Your coaches register their students from their own dashboard.
  4. 4 Everyone signs in at portals.html going forward.
  • School name and island

Frequently asked.

Who can actually sign in right now?

Students and coaches already in an MCC program, plus any school or coach we've set up a portal for. If that's not you yet, use the school or coach path above and we'll sort it out.

Do I need to install an app?

No — it's a website that works in any browser. You can optionally add it to your home screen for an app-like shortcut, but it's never required.

Is there a cost?

It depends on your program and setup. Email us with a bit of context and we'll walk you through it directly rather than guessing here.

I'm not sure if I need a school account or a coach account.

If you teach chess under a school's name, that's a school account. If you coach independently, under your own name, that's a coach account. Not sure either way — just email us and we'll point you in the right direction.

What happens after I email you?

We'll reply directly by email to confirm details and get your account set up, then send sign-in details for whoever needs them.
