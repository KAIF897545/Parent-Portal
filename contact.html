Get in touch.

Whether you're a school looking to bring the portal to your students, or a coach running your own lessons — here's exactly how to get started.

I'm a school

Email us with your school's name and the age groups you teach. We'll set up your school's portal and send login details for your coaches and students to sign in with.

Email about a school portal

I'm an independent coach

Email us to get a private coaching account set up under our Private Tutoring program. You'll be able to register your own students and track their progress the same way a school would.
